# KitaHack AI Judging Criteria & Grading Rubric

This document outlines the comprehensive grading rubric for the technology-driven projects focusing on Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and Google AI technologies.

## I. Scoring Overview
- **Total Raw Score:** 80 points
- **Final Score Calculation:** Raw Score × 1.25 (Total out of 100)
- **Pillars:**
  - **IMPACT (60%)**: Problem-solving, validation, and innovation.
  - **TECHNOLOGIES (20%)**: Architecture, implementation, and functional prototype.

---

## II. IMPACT (60 Points)

### 1. Problem Statement & SDG Alignment (15 pts)
*   **Excellent (12–15):** Strong, compelling problem; well-justified SDG based on real needs; AI/tech deeply integrated to solve the problem.
*   **Good (8–11):** Clear problem with justified SDG; relevant technology applied.
*   **Moderate (4–7):** Problem stated but generic; SDG loosely connected; basic tech mention.
*   **Weak (0–3):** Problem unclear; SDG poorly defined or incorrect; no meaningful tech usage.

### 2. User Feedback & Iteration (15 pts)
*   **Excellent (12–15):** Strong validation with real users; multiple insights; clear, well-documented iteration process.
*   **Good (8–11):** Clear user feedback with key insights; visible improvements made.
*   **Moderate (4–7):** Limited feedback; insights shallow; minimal iteration.
*   **Weak (0–3):** No real user feedback; no iteration shown.

### 3. Success Metrics & Scalability (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Strong impact metrics with data-driven goals; clear, realistic, and scalable roadmap.
*   **Good (6–7):** Clear, measurable goals; reasonable scaling plan.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Basic metrics or vague scalability ideas.
*   **Weak (0–2):** No clear metrics; no scalability plan.

### 4. AI Integration (Google AI Recommended) (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** AI is core to the solution; strong justification of Google AI choice (e.g., Gemini, Vertex AI) and clear benefits demonstrated.
*   **Good (6–7):** AI clearly improves solution; technology choice explained.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** AI included but adds limited value; weak explanation.
*   **Weak (0–2):** AI not used or unjustified.

### 5. Technology Innovation (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Highly creative and original solution; technology used in novel, impactful ways.
*   **Good (6–7):** Innovative approach with effective tech usage.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Some creative aspects; limited differentiation.
*   **Weak (0–2):** Solution is generic; minimal innovation.

---

## III. TECHNOLOGIES (20 Points)

### 6. Technical Architecture & Google Technologies (5 pts)
*   **Excellent (5):** Well-structured architecture; strong, well-reasoned use of Google technologies.
*   **Good (3–4):** Clear architecture with reasonable tech justification.
*   **Moderate (2):** Basic architecture shown; limited justification.
*   **Weak (0–1):** Architecture unclear; no justification of tech choices.

### 7. Technical Implementation & Challenges (5 pts)
*   **Excellent (5):** Fully implemented solution; major challenges clearly explained with strong technical decisions.
*   **Good (3–4):** Most components implemented; challenges explained.
*   **Moderate (2):** Partial implementation; challenges mentioned briefly.
*   **Weak (0–1):** Major components missing; challenges not addressed.

### 8. Completeness & Demonstration (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Fully functional, polished prototype; strong live demonstration using tools like Flutter, GitHub, etc.
*   **Good (6–7):** Functional prototype demonstrated successfully.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Partial prototype; limited demo.
*   **Weak (0–2):** No working prototype; unable to demo.
