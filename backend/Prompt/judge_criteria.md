# KitaHack AI Judging Criteria & Grading Rubric

This document outlines the comprehensive grading rubric for evaluating technology-driven projects.

## I. Scoring Overview
- **Total Raw Score:** 80 points
- **Final Score Calculation:** Raw Score × 1.25 (Total out of 100)
- **Pillars:**
  - **Technical Implementation and Architecture (40 pts)**: Google tech, AI quality, demo, and model performance.
  - **Business Innovation and Problem Solving (40 pts)**: Originality, problem fit, scalability, and deployment readiness.

---

## II. Technical Implementation and Architecture (40 Points)

### 1. Google Technology Integration (15 pts)
*   **Excellent (12–15):** Meaningful and integral use of at least one Google Developer technology; demonstrates clear understanding of why this technology was chosen and how it enhances the solution.
*   **Good (8–11):** Google technology clearly used with reasonable justification for the choice.
*   **Moderate (4–7):** Google technology present but peripheral; justification is weak or generic.
*   **Weak (0–3):** Google technology absent, superficial, or unjustified.

### 2. AI Implementation Quality (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** AI is essential to the solution (not decorative); appropriate model chosen with clear justification; ethical AI aspects considered (bias, hallucination mitigation, privacy, transparency).
*   **Good (6–7):** AI meaningfully improves solution; model choice reasonable; some ethical awareness shown.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** AI present but adds limited value; model choice not justified; ethics not addressed.
*   **Weak (0–2):** AI absent, unjustified, or purely cosmetic.

### 3. Working Demonstration & UI/UX (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Functional prototype demonstrating core features; stable execution with no critical or fatal bugs; clear and intuitive user experience.
*   **Good (6–7):** Functional prototype with minor issues; UX reasonably clear and usable.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Partial prototype; UX confusing or inconsistent; non-critical bugs present.
*   **Weak (0–2):** No working prototype or critical bugs make it unusable.

### 4. AI Model Performance (5 pts)
*   **Excellent (5):** Accuracy, efficiency, and effectiveness of AI components clearly demonstrated; evidence of concrete efforts to reduce hallucinations or incorrect outputs.
*   **Good (3–4):** AI performs adequately; some evidence of testing or hallucination mitigation.
*   **Moderate (2):** AI output quality inconsistent; limited evidence of optimization.
*   **Weak (0–1):** AI outputs unreliable or not evaluated.

---

## III. Business Innovation and Problem Solving (40 Points)

### 5. Originality and Creativity (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Novel or creative approach to addressing the problem; innovative use of AI and/or Google technologies; clear differentiation from existing solutions or approaches.
*   **Good (6–7):** Creative approach with meaningful differentiation; innovative tech use.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Some creative aspects; limited differentiation from existing solutions.
*   **Weak (0–2):** Generic solution; minimal innovation or differentiation.

### 6. Problem–Solution Fit & Real-World Relevance (15 pts)
*   **Excellent (12–15):** Well-defined real-world problem; clear identification of stakeholders and target beneficiaries; solution directly and practically addresses the problem in a realistic way.
*   **Good (8–11):** Clear problem with identified stakeholders; solution is practical and relevant.
*   **Moderate (4–7):** Problem stated but vague; stakeholders loosely defined; solution partially addresses the need.
*   **Weak (0–3):** Problem unclear or contrived; no identifiable stakeholders; solution does not match the problem.

### 7. Scalability (10 pts)
*   **Excellent (8–10):** Thoughtful consideration of scalability, cost, and resource usage; viable business model assessed with potential for sustainable, profitable growth.
*   **Good (6–7):** Reasonable scalability plan; business model has some viability.
*   **Moderate (3–5):** Basic scaling ideas or business model; not fully developed.
*   **Weak (0–2):** No scalability consideration; no viable business model.

### 8. Deployment Readiness (5 pts)
*   **Excellent (5):** Feasible deployment approach outlined (cloud, APIs, infrastructure); clear potential to evolve into a real-world product or startup.
*   **Good (3–4):** Deployment approach described with some feasibility; product evolution plausible.
*   **Moderate (2):** Deployment mentioned but vague; product potential unclear.
*   **Weak (0–1):** No deployment plan; no path to a real-world product.
